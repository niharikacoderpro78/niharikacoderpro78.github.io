Blinkit Sales Analysis

Tools Used

Power BI, Excel, Data Visualization

Key Insights

  • Tier-3 outlets generated the highest sales volume across multiple product categories.
  • Low-fat products showed consistent demand across different outlet types.
  • Supermarket Type-1 outlets contributed the highest share of total revenue.
  • Recently established outlets showed strong growth in sales performance.

Business Impact

The analysis helps retail businesses identify high-performing outlets and product categories. These insights support better inventory planning, product strategy decisions, and location-based expansion strategies.

Mobile Verse Analytics

Tools Used

Power BI, SQL, Excel

Key Insights

  • Apple and Samsung brands generated the highest sales revenue among all smartphone brands.
  • Premium smartphones showed stronger sales performance compared to mid-range models.
  • Online payment methods were the most commonly used transaction type among customers.
  • Customer rating trends revealed strong brand loyalty for top smartphone brands.

Business Impact

This analysis helps smartphone retailers understand customer purchasing patterns and brand performance. The insights can support better inventory management, targeted marketing campaigns, and strategic product positioning within competitive mobile markets.

Insurance Sales & Claims Analysis

Tools Used

SQL, Python (Pandas, Matplotlib), Excel

Key Insights

  • Claim costs increased significantly during specific months indicating seasonal risk patterns.
  • Certain policy categories generated higher claim frequencies compared to others.
  • Premium revenue remained stable while claim costs fluctuated across policy periods.
  • High claim ratio policies highlighted areas of potential financial risk exposure.

Business Impact

This analysis helps insurance companies evaluate financial risk exposure and improve policy pricing strategies. By understanding claim cost trends and claim frequency, insurers can adjust underwriting policies and optimize risk management decisions.
