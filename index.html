// about

Numbers that behave, stories that stay

I’m Niharika, a Finance & Data Analyst who believes every messy spreadsheet is just a story waiting for a better editor. I have cleaned duplicate rows, fixed date formats that could not agree on what year it was, and turned “where did the money go?” into a dashboard you can read over a cup of chai.

My method is simple: question first, data second, chart last. I work with SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI and Tableau, and I treat every number with healthy suspicion until it has passed a quality check. If a metric cannot change a decision, it does not get a chart. Fun fact: my favourite bug is the one that fails silently, because that is where the best detective work begins.

🧹
Rule 1: clean before you chartA beautiful chart on dirty data is still wrong, just prettier.
🔍
Rule 2: trust, then verifyOutliers, nulls and duplicates get questioned before they reach a report.
📊
Rule 3: one dashboard, one storySix sharp charts beat twenty confused ones.
💬
Rule 4: explain it in one sentenceIf I cannot say it simply, I have not understood it yet.
